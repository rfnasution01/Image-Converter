## Publishing notes

- CTA link used in this pack: `https://www.pixconvertly.site`. Update it if your production domain changes.
- Instagram: use the visuals as feed posts and create a cropped version for Stories or Reel covers.
- X: publish around 9:00–11:00 or 18:00–21:00 in your audience’s local time.
- LinkedIn: use short paragraphs and one clear CTA; keep hashtags limited to 3–5.
- For privacy claims, use wording such as “processed locally in your browser” and avoid absolute claims like “100% secure.”
